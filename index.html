About

Fashion design shaped by texture, materials, and mood.

Designs by Brooke is an emerging fashion portfolio exploring the relationship between fabric, silhouette, color, and personal style. The work focuses on material choices, texture combinations, soft neutral palettes, styling ideas, and visual storytelling.
